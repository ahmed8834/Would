# Privacy Policy — Hidden Word (لعبة الكلمة المخفية)

**Last updated:** July 19, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the mobile game **Hidden Word** ("the App", "we", "us") handles information when you use it. We built Hidden Word as a casual, family-friendly word-guessing game and we try to collect as little data as possible to make it work.

By downloading or using the App, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

---

## 1. Information We Collect

### 1.1 Information you provide directly
- **Player nickname** — the name you optionally enter to appear on the local leaderboard. This is stored **only on your device** and is never sent to us or to any server we control.

### 1.2 Information collected automatically
The App itself does not run its own analytics or tracking code. However, it uses the third-party service below to display ads, which may automatically collect certain technical data:

- **Google AdMob** (advertising): may collect your advertising ID, device identifiers, general device information (model, OS version), IP address / approximate location, and ad interaction data, in order to serve and measure banner, interstitial, and rewarded ads.
- **Google User Messaging Platform (UMP)**: used to request and record your consent choices (e.g., for personalized vs. non‑personalized ads) where required by law (such as under GDPR in the EEA/UK).

We do not receive or store this advertising data ourselves — it is collected and processed directly by Google in accordance with Google's own privacy policy:
👉 https://policies.google.com/privacy

### 1.3 Information stored locally on your device
The following data stays on your device only and is **not** transmitted to us:
- Your player nickname
- Game scores, best streaks, and leaderboard history
- Selected difficulty level and sound settings

Uninstalling the App permanently deletes this local data.

---

## 2. Permissions Used

The App requests the following Android permissions, used strictly for the purposes below:
- **Internet** — required to load ads and check connectivity.
- **Access Network State** — used to detect whether the device is online, so the App can show an offline notice and avoid failed ad requests.

The App does **not** request access to your contacts, camera, microphone, photos, location, or any other sensitive permission.

---

## 3. How We Use Information

Any data described above is used only to:
- Operate core gameplay (word puzzles, scoring, local leaderboard)
- Display and measure advertising, through Google AdMob
- Comply with applicable consent requirements (GDPR/UK GDPR, and similar laws) via Google UMP

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it with third parties other than the advertising service described above.

---

## 4. Children's Privacy

Hidden Word is designed for a general, family-friendly audience and does not knowingly target or collect personal information from children under the age of 13 (or the relevant age of digital consent in your country). Where required, ads are configured to run in a manner consistent with Google's policies for child-directed or mixed-audience content. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us using the details below so we can address it.

---

## 5. Data Retention

- Locally stored data (nickname, scores, settings) remains on your device until you uninstall the App or clear its data manually.
- Advertising-related data collected by Google AdMob is retained and managed according to Google's own retention practices.

---

## 6. Your Choices and Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete personal data, or to object to certain processing (for example, under GDPR if you are in the EEA/UK, or similar laws elsewhere). Because most gameplay data is stored only on your device, you can exercise these rights directly by:
- Clearing the App's data or uninstalling it from your device settings
- Adjusting your ad personalization / consent choices when prompted by the in-app consent form, or through your device's ad settings (**Settings → Google → Ads**)

For anything related to data Google processes on our behalf, you can also review Google's own controls at https://myadcenter.google.com.

---

## 7. Data Security

We rely on standard Android platform security for locally stored data, and on Google's security practices for any data processed through AdMob/UMP. No method of storage or transmission is 100% secure, but we do not operate any external server that stores your personal data.
